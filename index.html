Liftosaur Local

A personal, unofficial build of the open-source Liftosaur weightlifting tracker for Android. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Liftosaur project.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

Liftosaur Local keeps your workout data on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no server of its own, and it does not send your data to the app's developer or anyone else.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission. It uses that permission only to create, list, download and delete its own backup files in the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Backups go straight from your device to your Google account. The developer never receives them, and nothing obtained through Google APIs is shared, sold, or used for advertising. Liftosaur Local's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data from Google Drive (Drive settings, Manage apps) removes the backups.
